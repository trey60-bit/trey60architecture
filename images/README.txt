PHOTOS

The two big background photos load straight from Unsplash, so the site
works with this folder empty. To host a photo yourself (faster, and safe if
the original is ever removed), save it here with the matching name and it
takes over automatically:

hero.jpg      hero background    unsplash.com/photos/0yVSKLYx5do  (Ryoji Iwata, spiral staircase)
booking.jpg   booking section    unsplash.com/photos/vgoPSHAR5Yc  (Luciana Ribeiro, spiral atrium)

Want a different photo? Save any landscape JPG under one of those names.
Aim for 2000px wide and under 400 KB. Photos are shown in black and white
with a blue tint, so almost any architectural shot will match the palette.
